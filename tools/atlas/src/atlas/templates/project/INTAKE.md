# Day-1 intake - {{project_name}}

Send these to Tyler as one message on day 1, before anything else. Research and drafting
start at once; an answer overrides anything researched. "Unknown - go find it" turns an
item into a research question with a requirement and a 30 min time-box.

Answers go into `PROJECT.md` as facts. Anything still unconfirmed when drawn becomes a
`basis:` row, never sheet text.

1. Project address exactly as the County records it, and the APN.
2. Owner name, mailing address and phone; applicant or agent if different.
3. Preparer or firm name as printed; who signs; seal yes or no.
4. Numbers, each in its own field: firm project number, J-number, permit or application number.
5. Scope per structure: demolish or retain; floor area and former use of each structure being demolished.
6. Job start and completion dates to print.
7. Zoning setbacks (front, rear, side, road centre), or "pull from zoning".
8. Site features: creek, easement, wells (including within 100 ft), septic, tanks - present, none or unknown.
9. Utilities: each service and its disposition (cap, remove or protect); utility release status.
10. Records you hold: surveys, prior permits, septic records, photos - send them now.
11. AHJ, who submits, and any known checklist or format rules.
12. Sheet list, deadline, and anything the agency has already asked for.
