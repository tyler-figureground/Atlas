## Division 03 - Concrete

### Section 03 30 00 - Cast-in-Place Concrete

#### Part 1 - General

**1.01 Section Includes**
A. Cast-in-place concrete for the new stair infill and slab toppings.

**1.03 References**
A. ASTM C150/C150M, Standard Specification for Portland Cement.
B. ISO 14025, Environmental labels and declarations - Type III environmental declarations - Principles and procedures.
C. ISO 21930, Sustainability in buildings and civil engineering works - Core rules for environmental product declarations of construction products and services.

**1.04 Submittals**

D. Environmental Product Declarations:
   1. Submit product-specific Type III Environmental Product Declaration (EPD)
      conforming to ISO 14025 and ISO 21930.
   2. EPD shall be published by a program operator accredited per ISO 14025.
   3. EPD shall be current and valid at the time of submittal. Expired EPDs
      are not acceptable.
   4. EPD shall report environmental impacts for life cycle stages A1 through
      A3 (raw material supply, transport to manufacturer, manufacturing) at
      minimum.

**1.06 Sustainability Requirements**

A. Environmental Product Declaration Requirements:
   1. Provide products with Type III EPDs meeting the requirements of Article 1.04.D.
   2. Maximum Global Warming Potential (GWP):
      a. Ready-mix concrete, 4000 PSI: 350 kg CO2e per m3, measured for life
         cycle stages A1 through A3.
   3. GWP shall be calculated in accordance with ISO 21930 and reported using
      characterization factors from IPCC AR5 or later.

#### Part 2 - Products

**2.01 Manufacturers**
A. Basis-of-design: FableMix Ready Mix, or approved equal.
B. All manufacturers shall provide a current, valid Type III EPD meeting the
   requirements of Article 1.04.D. Manufacturers unable to provide a conforming
   EPD are not acceptable.

**2.03 Environmental Performance Requirements**

A. Maximum Global Warming Potential (GWP):
   1. Ready-mix concrete, 4000 PSI: Maximum 350 kg CO2e per m3, for life cycle
      stages A1 through A3.
   2. GWP value shall be as reported in the product-specific EPD submitted
      under Article 1.04.D.
