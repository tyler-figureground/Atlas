## Neighborhood History

### Development History
The corridor industrialized in the 1890s around the rail freight line, then filled with breweries, metal shops and print works through 1930. Postwar decline emptied the loft stock; artists and small manufacturers recolonized it from the 1980s, and the 2000s rezoning opened the side streets to residential conversion.
